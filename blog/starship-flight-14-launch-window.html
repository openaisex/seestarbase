Starship Flight 14: Road & Beach Closures Now Confirmed

Updated September 26, 2026

Update, September 26: SpaceX continues to target Flight 14 no earlier than Monday, September 28, 2026. The 75-minute window still opens at 7:15 a.m. CDT (12:15 UTC), pending regulatory approval — the standard caveat SpaceX attaches to every launch date until liftoff. A successful wet dress rehearsal was completed on September 24, with Booster 21 and Ship 41 stacked on Pad 2.

SpaceX has not yet published a revised backup date or a new FAA airspace advisory with an exact window-close time, so we won't guess at those details. We'll update this page as soon as official information is released.

What we know so far

This would be Starship's first attempt at a full orbital flight, and the first flight to deploy Starlink V3 satellites into orbit. SpaceX's mission page lists 26 Starlink V3 satellites as the payload.

  • Primary date: NET September 28, 2026
  • Window opens: 7:15 a.m. CDT (12:15 UTC), ~75 minutes
  • Backup date: not yet published by SpaceX
  • Vehicles: Booster 21 + Ship 41 from Pad 2
  • Payload: 26 Starlink V3 satellites

Road and beach status — now confirmed

The City of Starbase has issued formal closure orders. Primary closure: September 28, 2026, from 4:00 a.m. to 10:00 a.m. Alternate dates: September 29 and September 30, 2026, same hours (4:00 a.m. to 10:00 a.m.).

Closures cover Boca Chica Beach, Texas State Highway 4 (from FM 1419/Oklahoma Ave. to the beach entrance), and the associated FAA hazard areas/Clear Zone. Check the official City of Starbase notices at starbase.texas.gov and text "BEACH" to 866-513-3475 for real-time alerts before you travel.

Sources: SpaceX official X account and spacex.com Flight 14 mission page (schedule update September 17, wet dress rehearsal confirmation September 24, 2026); City of Starbase Mayor's Order No. 2026-23. Previous target of September 22–23 was based on an earlier FAA airspace advisory and NASASpaceflight reporting, now superseded by SpaceX's own announcements.
