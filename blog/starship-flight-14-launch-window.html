Starship Flight 14 now targeting NET September 22

September 15, 2026

Starship Flight 14 is currently targeted no earlier than Tuesday, September 22, 2026, according to the latest FAA airspace advisory. The primary window is listed as 12:15–14:14 UTC (7:15–9:14 a.m. CDT), with September 23 as the backup date.

What we know so far

This would be Starship's first attempt at a full orbital flight. The mission is expected to carry approximately 20 operational Starlink V3 satellites.

  • Primary date: NET September 22, 2026
  • Window: 7:15–9:14 a.m. CDT (12:15–14:14 UTC)
  • Backup: September 23, same window
  • Vehicles: Booster 21 + Ship 41 from Pad 2

SpaceX has not yet posted an official confirmation of the exact launch date. The current target comes from FAA planning documents. Dates for Starship flights often shift.

Road and beach status

As of September 15, the City of Starbase has not issued a formal Highway 4 or Boca Chica Beach closure order for Flight 14. Closures are typically published 2–5 days before a launch attempt. Check the official City of Starbase notices and text "BEACH" to 866-513-3475 for real-time alerts.

Sources: FAA airspace advisory (primary window updated to September 22–23); NASASpaceflight reporting on the schedule change; City of Starbase road closure status as of September 14–15, 2026. No official SpaceX confirmation of the exact date has been issued yet.
