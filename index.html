🔭 Best Starship Viewing Spots

Where to set up your camera and witness history unfold in Texas.

Distance: ~5 Miles

Isla Blanca Park (South Padre)

The most popular public spot across the jetties. Excellent line-of-sight view of the Orbital Launch Mount across the bay.

  • 📍 Location: South Padre Island Tip
  • 🚗 Parking: $10 County Park Fee
  • 🚽 Facilities: Restrooms & Pavilions
Distance: ~2 Miles

Boca Chica Highway (Highway 4)

Closest public road before security checkpoints close. Unrivaled sound impact during engine ignition.

  • 📍 Location: HWY 4 Checkpoint Area
  • 🚗 Parking: Roadside (Fills fast!)
  • ⚠️ Warning: Road closures apply
Distance: ~7 Miles

Port Isabel Boat Ramp

A great alternative for photographers wanting water reflections without crossing the South Padre Causeway traffic.

  • 📍 Location: Under Port Isabel Bridge
  • 🚗 Parking: Free/Limited
  • 📷 Vibe: Chill & Family-Friendly

Essential Transit Info

Car Rentals at BRO & HRL Airports

There is no public transit to Boca Chica Beach. Renting a car at Brownsville/South Padre Island International Airport (BRO) or Valley International Airport (HRL) is highly recommended.

🚗 BRO to Starbase: 25 mins
🚗 HRL to Starbase: 50 mins
